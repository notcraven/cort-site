SERVICES & FEES

Individual therapy for adults 18+, in person in Grand Blanc or by telehealth anywhere in Michigan.

INDIVIDUAL THERAPY

One-on-one sessions built around what you want to change and how you like to work.

Common reasons people come in

Anxiety and stress • Low mood • Burnout • Living with chronic illness • Life transitions • Relationships and family • Identity exploration • Sociopolitical stress

GENDER-AFFIRMING CARE

For trans, nonbinary, gender-expansive, and questioning folks, wherever you are with it.

What this can look like

Exploring and questioning • Social, medical, or legal transition support • Coming out at home, school, or work • Dysphoria and euphoria • Support for partners and family

Surgery letters: For self-pay telehealth clients, I write letters of support for gender-affirming surgery after we've worked together for at least six months. That time lets me know you well enough to write a thorough, specific letter that holds up with surgeons and insurers. If you need a letter sooner, or you're using insurance, I keep a list of trusted therapists who write them after a single session, and I'm glad to connect you.

NEURODIVERGENT-AFFIRMING

For autistic and ADHD adults, diagnosed, self-identified, or still wondering.

What this can look like

Late identification • Unmasking • Autistic burnout • Sensory overload • Naming and mapping emotions • Executive function supports that fit your life

GAME-INFORMED THERAPY

Games give us a shared language for hard things. I use them inside established clinical approaches.

What this can look like

Character sheets for your strengths • Goals framed as quests • Card-based reflection prompts • Talking through the games you already play

QUESTIONS PEOPLE ASK

What happens at a free consult?

A short phone or video call ([LENGTH] minutes) to see if we're a good fit. Ask me anything about how I work. No paperwork and no pressure.

Do I have to like video games?

Nope. Game-informed work is one tool among many. If games aren't your thing, we'll use the stories, hobbies, and language that fit you.

Can you diagnose autism or ADHD?

I don't provide autism or ADHD assessments. I work with you whether you're formally diagnosed, self-identified, or still wondering, and I can refer you to providers who do assessments.

What does LLPC mean?

Limited Licensed Professional Counselor. It's the Michigan license for counselors who have completed a graduate counseling degree and are finishing supervised hours toward full licensure. I run our sessions, and a licensed supervisor consults on my work.

What if I need help between sessions?

I'm not available for emergencies. If you're in crisis, call or text 988 or call 911. For scheduling questions, use the contact form.
