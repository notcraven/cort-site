INDIVIDUAL THERAPY

One-on-one therapy for adults, built around what you want to change and how you like to work.

WHAT IT IS

Individual therapy is time that’s yours: one person, one counselor, and a plan that fits you. We start by getting clear on what’s bringing you in and what you’d like to be different. From there, we shape the work around how you think and communicate.

I draw on CBT, DBT skills, mindfulness, and narrative therapy, and I work from a person-centered, feminist, and multicultural lens. In plain terms: you’re the expert on your life, the systems around you matter, and we use the tools that actually help.

Humor is welcome here. So are long pauses, tangents, and days when you don’t know where to start.

IN SESSION, IT MIGHT SOUND LIKE…

“What would ‘a little better’ look like by next month?”

“Let’s map what happened right before the spiral.”

“That’s a lot to carry. What’s one piece we can set down today?”

WHAT WE CAN WORK ON

ANXIETY AND STRESS

Understanding what sets it off, calming your body, and loosening the loops your mind gets stuck in.

LOW MOOD

Finding small, realistic ways back to energy and meaning when everything feels heavy.

BURNOUT

Sorting out what’s draining you, setting limits, and rebuilding at a pace you can keep.

LIFE TRANSITIONS

Moves, breakups, career changes, new diagnoses, and other times the map stops matching the road.

RELATIONSHIPS AND FAMILY

Communication, boundaries, conflict, and figuring out what you need from the people around you.

IDENTITY AND SOCIOPOLITICAL STRESS

Exploring who you are, and coping when the news and the world feel like too much.

WHO IT'S FOR

  • Adults 18 and older anywhere in Michigan.
  • People trying therapy for the first time, and people whose last therapist wasn’t a fit.
  • Anyone who wants a counselor who’s direct, warm, and doesn’t flinch.
  • Folks who like structure, folks who’d rather talk it out, and everyone in between.

COMMON QUESTIONS

What happens in the first session?

First, we’ll have a free 20-minute video consult to see if we’re a good fit. Your first full session is an intake, where we talk about what brings you in, your history, and what you’d like to get out of therapy. You’re welcome to ask me questions too.

How often will we meet?

Most people start weekly. As things improve, many move to every other week. We decide together.

Can I see you in person?

Yes. I see clients in person in Grand Blanc on Tuesdays and Thursdays through Crescent Counseling, and by telehealth anywhere in Michigan.
