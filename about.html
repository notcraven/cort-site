HI, I'M CORT.

I'm a trans and autistic counselor in Grand Blanc, Michigan, and I want therapy to feel like a conversation with someone firmly in your corner. I specialize in neurodivergent-affirming and gender-affirming care for adults 18+, and I know how much it matters to find a therapist you don't have to educate.

I work from a person-centered, feminist, and multicultural lens. You're the expert on your own life, and we'll look at the systems and pressures around you along with what's happening inside.

Before becoming a counselor, I spent 15 years working in tech. Now I bring the things I love into the room when they help: games, stories, and a lot of curiosity about how you see the world.

HOW I WORK

01

Affirming from the first session. Your identity is not a diagnosis. It's context for our work, and you won't have to explain or defend it.

02

Collaborative. We set goals together and revisit them, so you always know where we're headed.

03

Skills when they help. I draw on narrative therapy, solution-focused and strength-based approaches, CBT, DBT skills, mindfulness, and game-informed interventions.

04

Plain language. I'll tell you what we're doing and why. Direct questions get direct answers.

05

Sensory-friendly. Fidgets, stimming, pacing, and breaks are all welcome. Tell me what your nervous system needs.
