HI, I'M CORT.

I'm a counselor in Grand Blanc, Michigan, and I want therapy to feel like a conversation with someone firmly in your corner. I specialize in neurodivergent-affirming and gender-affirming care for adults 18+.

I bring the things I love into the room when they help: games, stories, and a lot of curiosity about how you see the world.
