## What it is

Gender-affirming therapy starts from a simple premise: your gender is yours to define, and you don't have to prove it to me. There's no gatekeeping and no checklist to pass. We focus on what you want, whether that's room to explore, support through a transition, or help with everything happening around you.

I'm a trans man, so I know a lot of this from the inside. I also know my experience isn't yours, and I'll ask before I assume.

> You don't need to have it figured out. Questioning is a perfectly good place to start.

## [aside] In session, it might sound like

- "What's felt like euphoria lately, even in small ways?"
- "Who in your life is safe to tell, and who can wait?"
- "Let's plan for the appointment you've been dreading."

## [cards] What we can work on

- **Exploring and questioning** Space to try on names, pronouns, and presentations with no pressure to land anywhere.
- **Transition support** Social, medical, or legal steps, at your pace and in your order.
- **Coming out** Planning who to tell, how, and what to do if it goes badly, at home, at work, or at school.
- **Dysphoria and euphoria** Getting through the hard days and noticing what makes you feel most like yourself.
- **Minority stress** Handling misgendering, discrimination, and the news cycle without letting them run your life.
- **Partners and family** Support for the people who love you as they learn, adjust, and figure out how to show up.

## [checks] Who it's for

- Trans, nonbinary, and gender-expansive adults.
- Anyone questioning their gender, with no label required.
- Partners, parents, and family members of trans people.
- People who've had to educate past therapists about their own lives.

## [faq] Common questions

### Do you write letters for gender-affirming surgery?

For self-pay telehealth clients, I write letters of support after we've worked together for at least six months. If you need a letter sooner, or you see me through Crescent Counseling, I keep a list of trusted therapists who write them after a single session, and I'm glad to connect you. [Read the full answer](services.html#surgery-letters).

### Will you diagnose me with gender dysphoria?

Only when it's needed, such as for a surgery letter. You don't need a diagnosis to work with me.

### Do I have to talk about gender?

No. Plenty of trans clients come in for anxiety, burnout, or relationships, and gender comes up only when it's relevant. You decide what we focus on.
