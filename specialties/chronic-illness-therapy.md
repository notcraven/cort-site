## What it is

Chronic illness touches everything: work, relationships, plans, identity, and how much energy is left at the end of the day. Therapy can be a place to talk about all of it, including the parts the people around you are tired of hearing.

I live with chronic illnesses myself. I won't tell you to think positive or suggest yoga as a cure. We'll focus on the life you have now and how to make it livable, meaningful, and yours.

> Your symptoms are real. Therapy is a place to work on the weight of carrying them.

## [aside] In session, it might sound like

- "What did this flare cost you, and what helped even a little?"
- "Let's budget your energy for the week before something else spends it."
- "What would you say to your doctor if you knew they'd listen?"

## [cards] What we can work on

- **Grief and loss** Grieving the life you expected while building one that works now.
- **Pacing your energy** Planning around crashes, protecting your good days, and saying no without a ten-minute explanation.
- **Flares and uncertainty** Coping with unpredictable symptoms and the anxiety of not knowing what tomorrow looks like.
- **Being doubted by providers** Processing medical dismissal and getting ready to advocate for yourself at appointments.
- **Invisible illness** Handling "but you don't look sick," disclosure at work, and people who don't get it.
- **Identity and self-worth** Separating your value from your productivity in a culture that measures people by output.

## [checks] Who it's for

- Adults living with chronic illness, chronic pain, or disability.
- People still searching for a diagnosis.
- Anyone tired of explaining their illness before they can talk about their life.
- Partners and caregivers of people with chronic illness.

## [faq] Common questions

### Can we meet by telehealth on flare days?

Yes. If you usually see me in person, we can switch to telehealth when getting to the office would cost too much.

### What if I'm too sick for a session?

I'm flexible about illness. Let me know as soon as you can, and we'll reschedule or switch to telehealth.

### Will therapy focus only on my illness?

Only as much as you want. Some people talk about their illness every week. Others want one hour where it isn't the main topic.
