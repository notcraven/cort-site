## What it is

Neurodivergent-affirming therapy starts from the view that autistic and ADHD brains work differently and deserve support on their own terms. The goal is a life that fits how you're wired. We won't spend sessions training you to look more neurotypical.

I'm autistic, and I wrote *Decoding Emotion: A Pattern-Based Guide for Autistic People*. I communicate directly, say what I mean, and won't make you guess what I'm thinking. You're welcome to stim, fidget, skip eye contact, take breaks, or info-dump about your special interest.

> Self-identified is valid. You don't need a formal diagnosis to work with me.

## [aside] In session, it might sound like

- "What does your body do right before a shutdown?"
- "Let's build a routine around how your brain actually works."
- "Tell me about the special interest. I'm listening."

## [cards] What we can work on

- **Late identification** Making sense of your history with new information, including the grief, relief, and anger that can come with it.
- **Unmasking** Sorting out which parts of you are performance and which are you, and where it's safe to drop the mask.
- **Autistic burnout** Recognizing the signs, cutting demands, and recovering without rushing back to the pace that burned you out.
- **Sensory overload** Mapping your sensory needs and building spaces and plans that respect them.
- **Naming emotions** Pattern-based tools for identifying and describing feelings, especially when they're hard to read from the inside.
- **Executive function** Practical supports for starting, switching, and finishing tasks, designed around your actual life.

## [checks] Who it's for

- Autistic, ADHD, and AuDHD adults.
- Adults identified later in life, or still wondering.
- People worn out from masking at work, at home, or with friends.
- Anyone who's had therapy that tried to make them more "normal."

## [faq] Common questions

### Can you diagnose autism or ADHD?

I don't provide autism or ADHD assessments. I work with you whether you're formally diagnosed, self-identified, or still wondering, and I can refer you to providers who do assessments.

### What's Decoding Emotion?

It's my book, a pattern-based guide to understanding emotions written for autistic people. Some clients use it alongside therapy, but you don't need to read it to work with me. It's available in [paperback](https://www.amazon.com/Decoding-Emotion-Pattern-Based-Autistic-People/dp/B0DS95QVDZ) and [Kindle](https://www.amazon.com/Decoding-Emotion-Pattern-Based-Autistic-People-ebook/dp/B0DS95QMBR).

### What if I'm not sure I'm neurodivergent?

That's a common place to start. We can explore what fits, what doesn't, and what would help, with or without a label.
