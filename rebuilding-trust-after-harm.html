REBUILDING TRUST AFTER HARM FROM MEN

For women who’ve been hurt by men and want to feel safe letting someone close again.

WHAT IT IS

A lot of the women I work with have been hurt by men. Some of that harm came from partners, some from family, and some from people who were supposed to keep them safe. Many of them want to feel safe around men again, and to stop bracing every time they let one get close.

As a trans man, I’m often a starting point for that work: a man in the room who understands why trust has to be earned. You set the pace and decide what you share. This work doesn’t require you to forgive anyone or to date anyone.

You don’t owe me trust on day one. We build it the same way you’ll build it with anyone else: slowly, and on your terms.

IN SESSION, IT MIGHT SOUND LIKE…

“What does your body tell you when he walks into the room?”

“What would someone need to do to earn the next step?”

“Let’s practice that boundary out loud.”

WHAT WE CAN WORK ON

FEELING SAFE IN YOUR BODY

Noticing the signals your body sends and learning to respond to them instead of overriding them.

BOUNDARIES

Deciding what you will and won’t accept, and practicing how to say it.

TRUSTING YOUR JUDGMENT

Rebuilding confidence in your read on people after someone taught you to doubt it.

VULNERABILITY AT YOUR PACE

Choosing who gets to see the tender parts of you, and when.

RELATIONSHIPS AND DATING

Carrying what you learn into friendships, family, work, and romance, if romance is something you want.

SHAME AND SELF-BLAME

Setting down the story that what happened was your fault.

WHO IT'S FOR

  • Women and nonbinary adults who’ve been hurt by men.
  • People who feel on guard around men and want that to change.
  • Survivors who want a trauma-informed approach where they stay in control.
  • Anyone curious whether working with a man could feel different this time.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Do I have to talk about what happened?

No. You decide what you share and when. Much of this work can happen without going through every detail.

What if I'm not comfortable working with a man?

That’s completely valid, and finding the right fit matters more than anything. The free consult is a low-stakes way to see how it feels. If it isn’t right, I’ll help you find someone who is.

Is this only for women?

Most of the people I see for this are women. Anyone who’s been hurt by men and wants to rebuild trust is welcome.
